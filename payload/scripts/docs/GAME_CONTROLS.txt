## GInput for GTA2 - List of controls

### A/0:
- Previous Weapon: L2
- Next Weapon: L1
- Special: Circle
- Brake/Jump: R1
- Turn Left/Right: DPadLeft DPadRight
- Forward: Cross
- Backward: Square
- Attack/Fire: R2
- Enter/Exit Car: Triangle
